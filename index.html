MIND56 Studio — Professional Photography Bangkok

MIND56 Studio is a professional photography studio based in Bangkok, Thailand, with over 12 years of experience delivering high-impact images for business professionals, executives, and corporate clients. We specialize in creating polished, credible visual identities through portrait photography, executive profile shoots, and business imagery designed to build trust and authority.

Our clients include insurance executives, financial advisors, and business leaders across Thailand's leading companies — including Allianz Ayudhya, Prudential, FWD, Muang Thai Life, Viriyah Insurance, and more. Whether you need a profile photo for LinkedIn, a corporate headshot for your company website, or a full executive portrait session, MIND56 Studio delivers results that elevate your professional image.

Based in central Bangkok with a fully equipped studio featuring natural-light setups, we offer both on-site sessions and location shoots. Our team understands the unique visual demands of Thailand's insurance and financial services industry, where a single powerful portrait can significantly influence trust and client conversion.

At MIND56 Studio, we believe that great photography is not just about technical skill — it is about storytelling. Every portrait tells a story of confidence, competence, and professionalism. Our photography services are tailored for individuals and teams who want their first impression to count, whether online or in print materials.
